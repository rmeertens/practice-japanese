Japanese Practice — Genki I & II

Tokidoki is a free, no-login collection of Japanese practice exercises built around the Genki textbook series. Every exercise is scheduled with spaced repetition (SRS) so you review what you need most, not what you already know.

Verb conjugation drills

Work through chapters 3–23 of Genki I and II, covering masu-form, て-form, ta-form, nai-form, tai-form, potential, volitional, passive, causative, ba-conditional, causative-passive, and more — for both godan (u-verb) and ichidan (ru-verb) patterns, plus the irregular verbs する and くる.

Adjective conjugation drills

Practise い-adjective and な-adjective conjugations including present, negative, past, past-negative, te-form, and adverb forms, chapter by chapter.

Build Your Own session

Mix and match any combination of verbs, adjectives, and conjugation forms into a custom practice session. Useful for targeting weak spots or reviewing before a test.

Sentence translation practice

Translate full Japanese sentences taken from Genki chapters 3–12. Toggle between Japanese-to-English and English-to-Japanese modes, with optional furigana readings above kanji.

Hiragana & katakana drawing practice

Learn the kana with a hands-on drawing drill: see a romaji prompt, draw the matching hiragana or katakana character by hand, then reveal the answer to check yourself. Covers the full gojuon, dakuten/handakuten, and yōon combinations for both scripts.

Printable JLPT kanji practice sheets

Free printable PDF worksheets for testing kanji recall by hand, one set per JLPT level from N5 through N1. Each sheet has one empty box per kanji with its English meaning above.
